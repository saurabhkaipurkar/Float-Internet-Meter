Privacy Policy

Float Internet Meter  |  Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Overview

Float Internet Meter ("the App", "we", "our") is an Android utility that shows your live internet speed in a floating window, in the status bar, and in a home-screen widget. It also shows how much Wi-Fi and mobile data your device has used. The App is published by DEVELOPER_NAME ("the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains what the App accesses on your device, how that information is used, and the choices you have.

In short: the App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and does not request the Internet permission. Everything the App uses is processed and stored only on your device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any information from you. We do not operate any server that the App talks to, so we never receive data from your device.

  • No personal information — no name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • No location data — the App does not use GPS or location services.
  • No app or browsing history — the App does not read which apps you use, which apps are installed, which websites you visit, or how much data each app uses.
  • No advertising identifiers — the App has no ad or tracking libraries.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting libraries inside the App.

3. Information the App Uses on Your Device

To do its job, the App reads and keeps the following on your phone. None of it leaves your device.

Data Purpose Stored?
Live network speed (bytes sent and received, from Android's system counters) Calculate and show your current download and upload speed. Not stored, except the latest reading, which the home-screen widget needs.
Connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile) Show which network you are on. Latest value only, for the widget.
Daily device-wide totals of Wi-Fi and mobile data used Show the Network Usage tab (today, 7 days, this month, 30 days). Yes, in the App's private database on your phone. The tab shows up to the last 31 days.
Your settings (theme, colors, font, text size, speed unit, toggles, position of the floating window) Remember how you set up the App. Yes, in the App's private storage on your phone.
The data usage totals are for the whole device. They do not say which app used the data.

4. Permissions We Request & Why

Permission Purpose
Display over other apps Show the floating speed window on top of other apps. Optional. You grant it in Android Settings and can take it back at any time.
Usage access Ask Android for the device-wide Wi-Fi and mobile data totals shown in the Network Usage tab. The App uses it only for these totals. It does not read which apps you use, for how long, or how much data each app uses. Optional: without it, live speed still works and only the Network Usage tab is unavailable.
Notifications Show your speed in the status bar and notification shade, and show the notification Android requires while the App runs in the background. Optional on Android 13 and later.
Foreground service (special use) Keep the speed meter running while you use other apps. Android shows a notification while it runs.
Run at startup Start the speed meter again after your phone restarts or the App is updated, if you left it on. You can turn this off in Settings → Start on boot.
Network state Tell whether you are on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

The App also offers an optional Quick Settings tile. It only switches the floating window on or off and needs no extra data.

None of these permissions are used to collect, log, or send your personal data anywhere. The App does not request the Internet permission.

5. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to show you your speed and data usage and to remember your settings. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose, and we do not combine it with any other data.

6. Storage, Security & Retention

  • All data is kept in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read under normal Android protections.
  • The App does not turn on Android's automatic backup for its data, so it is not copied to cloud backups by the App.
  • We run no backend server, so there is no remote database of your data that could be breached.
  • Data stays on your device until you delete it. See "Your Rights & Choices" below.

7. Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with anyone, because we do not have it. The App contains no third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics.

Some buttons hand you over to other apps, only when you tap them:

  • Share App opens the Android share sheet, and the app you pick receives only the link text.
  • Rate Us opens Google Play.
  • Contact Developer opens your email app. We receive only what you choose to send.
  • Privacy Policy and Terms open in your web browser.

Those apps have their own privacy policies. Separately, Google Play and Android may collect technical information (for example crash reports, if you allowed it on your device) under Google's own policies. We do not receive personal information from this.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Take back "Display over other apps", "Usage access", or "Notifications" at any time in Android Settings. The App then loses only the related feature.
  • Open the App → Settings → Reset All Data to erase your settings and stop the meter.
  • Android Settings → Apps → Float Internet Meter → Storage → Clear data removes everything the App stored, including the saved daily totals.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all data it stored on your device.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export, or delete. If you still have a question or request, write to us using the contact details below.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, whatever their age, because it collects no personal data.

10. Applicable Laws

This policy is written to meet Google Play's User Data policy and with applicable privacy laws in mind, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, where they apply, the GDPR and CCPA. Because the App does not collect or share personal data, most obligations about collecting, sharing, or selling data do not arise.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes or the law requires it. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will make it clear in the App or on the Google Play listing.

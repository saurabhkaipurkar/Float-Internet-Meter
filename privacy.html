Privacy Policy

Float Internet Meter  |  Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Overview

Float Internet Meter ("the App", "we", "our") is an Android utility that displays a real-time floating bubble and home-screen widget showing your device's live internet upload and download speed. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

In short: the App does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data to us or to any third party. All speed measurements are calculated and displayed entirely on your device.

2. What We Collect

The App does not require an account, does not use analytics SDKs, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not connect to any remote server operated by us.

  • No personal information — we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contact list.
  • No location data — the App does not access GPS or location services.
  • No browsing or usage history — we do not log which apps or websites generate the network traffic being measured.
  • No advertising identifiers — the App contains no ad network or tracking library.

3. Permissions We Request & Why

Permission Purpose
Network State Access Read live network traffic byte counters from the Android system to calculate current upload/download speed.
Display Over Other Apps Required to show the floating speed bubble on top of other apps and your home screen.
Foreground Service Keeps the speed monitor running reliably while the bubble/widget is active, in compliance with Android background-execution limits.
Notifications Shows the persistent status notification required by Android for foreground services, and lets you control the floating widget from the notification shade.
Internet Used only to measure the volume of data your device sends/receives — the App itself does not make network requests to any server.
None of these permissions are used to collect, log, or transmit your personal data. They exist solely to power the on-device speed-measurement and overlay features.

4. How Information Is Used

Network traffic statistics (bytes sent/received) are read directly from Android's system-level network counters in real time, used only to calculate and display your current speed, and are never written to a file, database, or sent anywhere. Once the App is closed or the calculation moment passes, that data point is discarded.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • App preferences (such as bubble position, size, color, and widget style) are stored locally on your device only, using Android's private app storage.
  • This preference data is never transmitted off your device and is automatically removed if you uninstall the App.
  • We do not operate any backend server, so there is no remote database that could be breached.

6. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics companies, because no such data is collected in the first place. The App contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age, since it collects no personal data at all.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • You can revoke the "Display over other apps" or "Notifications" permission at any time from Android Settings; the App will simply lose the related feature.
  • You can clear all locally stored preferences by clearing the App's storage/cache from Android Settings → Apps.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored data associated with it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's functionality or legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
